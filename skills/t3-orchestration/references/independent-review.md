# Independent review

1. Identify a stable candidate: base and candidate revisions, scoped diff including untracked files, acceptance checks and known failures. Copies or hash audits need a concrete integrity risk or requirement.
2. Use one authorized reviewer, without nested reviewers. Supply the diff, connected interfaces and evidence. Require severity, reproduction, smallest fix and an APPROVE/CHANGES verdict. Require the project's writing-tests guidance for testing work. Rerun only checks needed to resolve uncertainty; label inherited evidence.
3. Verify findings and fix small issues directly. Record fixed, disputed and declined findings in the existing task record. Run affected checks.
4. Recheck material repairs or unresolved findings with a targeted follow-up. Cosmetic changes and declined nits need no round. After two passes without material progress, reassess; another pass needs a concrete risk and expected new evidence. Honor explicitly required review workflows.
5. Close when required findings and checks are resolved. Optional nits need not block completion. Record the parent's disposition without inventing reviewer approval. Timeouts, task limits and unavailable reviewers leave required review open; continue independent authorized work.
